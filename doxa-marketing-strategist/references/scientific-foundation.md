# Scientific Foundation
Status: Research backlog — not yet canonical evidence

## Purpose
Build a defensible bridge between established brand science and AI Brand Perception.

## Candidate research areas
These are research directions, not adopted doctrine:
- Brand associations and associative memory
- Mental availability
- Category entry points
- Brand salience
- Distinctiveness and differentiation
- Consumer decision-making
- Information processing and heuristics
- Source credibility and trust
- Signaling theory
- Social proof
- Relevant knowledge representation / retrieval concepts in AI systems

## Adoption standard
Before using a scientific model as a DOXA proof claim:
1. identify primary or authoritative academic sources
2. state what the model actually claims
3. separate evidence from popular interpretation
4. explain its relevance to AI Brand Perception
5. explain where analogy to AI may break
6. decide whether it changes methodology or only helps explain it

Do not describe DOXA as scientifically validated merely because established theories are referenced.
