# Research Evidence Registry
Status: Empty scaffold / to be populated

## Purpose
Store evidence that supports DOXA's external claims without contaminating canonical strategy with volatile data.

## Entry template
### Claim
Exact claim the evidence supports.

### Source
Author / organization / title / date / URL or internal source.

### Evidence
Relevant figure, finding or observation.

### Scope
Market, geography, audience, platform, methodology and limitations.

### DOXA use
Where the evidence may be used: sales, website, newsletter, whitepaper, presentation, internal only.

### Confidence
High / medium / low.
