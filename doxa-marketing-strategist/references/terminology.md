# DOXA Terminology
Status: Controlled reference

## GEO
Generative Engine Optimization. Useful market/category term. DOXA treats the strategic task as broader than technical SEO.

## AI Visibility
Whether and how a brand appears in AI answers. Useful for audits, benchmarks, measurement and monitoring. It is not the ultimate strategic objective.

## AI Brand Perception
How AI systems understand, associate, classify, evaluate and recommend a brand. This describes DOXA's larger strategic problem.

## AI as a new target group
A strategic explanatory model, not a claim that the human customer stops being the brand's ultimate target.

Preferred clarification:
> Die Zielgruppe einer Marke bleibt der Mensch. Aber wer den Menschen erreichen will, muss zunehmend auch die KI erreichen, die ihn informiert, berät und Empfehlungen für ihn ausspricht.

## Readability
Can AI understand the brand?

## Visibility
Can AI find sufficient relevant signals about the brand?

## Credibility
Does AI have sufficient reasons or independent signals to believe/corroborate the brand picture?

## Handlungsfähigkeit
The customer understands where the brand stands, why, what needs to change, who should act and how impact will be measured.
